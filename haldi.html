A Morning Washed in Gold

The celebrations open with haldi — a paste of turmeric, sandalwood and rose water, blessed and then pressed gently onto the bride and groom by the hands of those who love them most.

It is the oldest kind of blessing: turmeric for protection, for a glow that no jeweller can match, and for the quiet promise that the family will always stand close enough to touch.

Come in yellow. Come ready to be stained. Nothing washes off a morning like this one.
