One Last Night by the Water

The lawns run down to Lake Pichola, and on the far shore the city palace will be lit. Dinner is served late, the way it should be, and the newly married couple will finally get to sit down.

Come to eat well, to raise a glass, and to tell the story of how you know them — everyone will be asked at least once.

Four days of ceremony end here, under the open sky. Stay as long as you like.
