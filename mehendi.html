Henna by Afternoon, Music by Dark

The courtyard fills early, while the light is still warm. Cones of henna move across palms and forearms in vines and paisleys, and somewhere in the pattern the groom's name is hidden for him to find.

As the mehendi dries, the sangeet begins — the dholak, the old family songs that nobody admits to knowing all the words to, and the dances both sides have been rehearsing in secret for weeks.

Wear something you can dance in. This is the night the two families stop being guests at each other's party.
