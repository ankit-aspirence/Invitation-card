Seven Steps, One Promise

At dusk the baraat arrives at the water's edge, and the mandap is lit. Beneath it, before the sacred fire and before everyone who raised them, Darshan and Dharal will take the saat phere — seven turns, each one a vow spoken aloud.

Nourishment. Strength. Prosperity. Happiness. Family. Harmony through every season. And friendship, for as long as they both shall live.

The fire is the only witness that cannot be sweet-talked. Please be seated before six.
